Lightyear Aperture Free, open verifier

Let the light in. Check our evidence yourself.

Aperture is our open verifier: an opening anyone outside Lightyear can use to check our work. Point it at any public evidence pack. It’s standard-library Python, needs no network and never runs our code. It passes only what it can anchor, and says “incomplete” rather than guessing.

Get Aperture 
$ python3 -I verify_public_evidence.py \
    all PUBLIC_EVIDENCE --strict --json

  0  PASS        every check anchored and passed
  1  FAIL        something doesn’t match
  3  INCOMPLETE  nothing it could anchor; no pass

How it works

From legacy unknown to signed verdict.

  1. STEP 1

    Map and lock the rules

    The knowledge graph maps programs, data and business rules, then the rules are frozen before anyone writes code.

  2. STEP 2

    Anyone builds

    Lightyear Factory, your AI platform or your SI. The builder never sees the hold-out answers.

  3. STEP 3

    Lightyear Prism judges

    Old and new run side by side, read-only, on our infrastructure, yours or a partner’s. Outputs, data and errors are compared field by field.

  4. STEP 4

    You get signed evidence

    Named differences, a risk figure for untested logic, and an evidence pack anyone can replay.
