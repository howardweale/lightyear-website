How we say no

“Indeterminate” is an answer.

When the evidence isn’t there, we say so. An empty output, a missing file or an untested branch never turns into a pass. That refusal is the reason our passes mean something.

EQUIVALENT Every compared field, record and error matched across the declared scenarios.
DIFFERENT We name the field, the record and the rule. A person decides whether it’s acceptable, and that decision is signed too.
INDETERMINATE Not enough evidence to decide. We say what’s missing and how to close it, and we never guess.
