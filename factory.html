How it works

Three AI roles work in a loop around a judge with no AI in it.

Lightyear Factory’s advantage is built in: nothing it writes counts until Lightyear Prism, the independent judge, says so. The judge is a deterministic program, not a model, and the agents never see its answer key. Prefer your own builder? The same judge verifies that too.
