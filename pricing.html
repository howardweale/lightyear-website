Your risk number

Know your true risk before you sign a multi-year SI contract.

In four weeks we report how much of your core business logic has never been exercised by any test you own. That one number reshapes scope, budget and vendor negotiation. Captured production traffic covers the happy path; rejection and error paths are where defects survive to go-live.

How the four weeks run

  1. Week 1. Agree scope and what “the same” means. Connect read-only.
  2. Week 2. Run the original and capture what it does.
  3. Week 3. Run the replacement on the same inputs and compare every field.
  4. Week 4. Signed evidence pack, risk number, business case and a plain-language readout.

What we need from you

Read-only access to the source, its inputs and outputs (or a way to run it on test data), the replacement if you have one, and one person who decides what “the same” means for your business.

Questions we get asked

Frequently asked

Do you compete with code-generation platforms?

No. We sit underneath them. Generation keeps getting cheaper; a better model should change your throughput, not your confidence. We decide whether generated change is safe to switch on, whoever wrote it.

What do you need from us to start?

Read-only access to source and enough captured input and output to be representative. If it isn’t representative, we’ll tell you, and that’s often the most valuable finding of the first month.

How is this different from a golden-master test suite?

A golden master compares against outputs someone chose to capture. We measure which decision paths that capture reaches, then manufacture inputs to close the gaps, usually the rejection and error paths.

Does the comparison report contain sensitive data?

Yes, by design: the values are the evidence. Classification, access control and retention are agreed with your security team before the first capture.

What happens after the programme ends?

It stays. Most customers keep the harness running against the estate, where it stops being a cost line and becomes infrastructure that catches regressions for years.
