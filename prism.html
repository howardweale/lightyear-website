Lightyear Prism · the independent judge

The judge that lets you ship AI-written code.

It runs the old system and the new one on the same inputs, compares every field and signs a verdict your auditor can replay. It judges work from any builder: Lightyear Factory, your AI platform or your systems integrator. It is a fixed program, tested before use, not an AI model.

The answer most tools cannot give

Three verdicts, not two.

A tool with only pass or fail has to pick one even when it has verified nothing, and it picks pass. The third verdict is the reason the first two can be trusted.

EQUIVALENT · EXIT 0

Both systems produced the same result, within the policy you approved. The release can proceed.

DIVERGENT · EXIT 1

They differ. The receipt names the field and the cause, so your team fixes the right thing first.

INDETERMINATE · EXIT 2

We could not verify this, and we will not pretend otherwise. Nothing is certified on missing evidence.
